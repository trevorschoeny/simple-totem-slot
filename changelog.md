## 1.0.0

First release.

### Added

- A slot above the offhand for a totem of undying, which saves you after a totem in either hand.
- A totem icon beside the hotbar while the slot holds a totem.
- Use totems anywhere in your inventory, off by default, with options to also search shulker boxes, bundles, and the ender chest.
- With Inventory Plus, Restock refills the slot and the settings are on a Totem Slot tab.
- With Simple Elytra Slot, the two slots share one column above the offhand.

Requires MenuKit 7.3.0 or newer and SlotKit 2.0.0 or newer.
